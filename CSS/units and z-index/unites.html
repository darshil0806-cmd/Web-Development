Absolute Units Absolute units are fixed — they do not scale with the screen size or parent element. They are best suited for print layouts, but in web design, we usually avoid them except for pixels (px). cm – Centimeters (1 cm = 37.8 px) mm – Millimeters (1 mm = 1/10th of 1 cm) in – Inches (1 in = 96 px = 2.54 cm) pt – Points (1pt = 1/72 in) pc – Picas (1pc = 12pt = 1/6 in) px – Pixels (1px = 1/96 in) → Most commonly used on screens Pixels (px) are most widely used because they directly map to screen dots
